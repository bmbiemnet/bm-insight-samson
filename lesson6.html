Lesson 6: The 8 Question Words

Crucial interrogative terms used to construct basic "Wh-" inquiries in Tigrinya.

Core "Wh-" Words

WHEN?
መዓስ፧
(meas?)
መኣስ
(meAs?)
Example Sentence
When are you coming?
መዓስ ክትመጽእ ኢኻ፧
meas kt-metsi ikha?
When are you coming?
መዓስ ክትመጺ ኢኺ፧
meas kt-metsi ikhi?
WHERE?
ኣበይ፧
(abey?)
Example Sentence
Where are you?
ኣበይ ኣለኻ፧
abey alekha?
Where are you?
ኣበይ ኣለኺ፧
abey alekhi?
WHY?
ስለምንታይ፧
(slemintay?)
Example Sentence
Why are you laughing?
ስለምንታይ ትስሕቕ ኣለኻ፧
slemintay tisihq alekha?
Why are you laughing?
ስለምንታይ ትስሕቂ ኣለኺ፧
slemintay tisihqi alekhi?
WHO?
መን፧
(men?)
Example Sentence
Who are you?
መን ኢኻ፧
men ikha?
Who are you?
መን ኢኺ፧
men ikhi?
WHAT?
እንታይ፧
(entay?)
Example Sentence
What are you doing?
እንታይ ትገብር ኣለኻ፧
entay tigebr alekha?
What are you doing?
እንታይ ትገብሪ ኣለኺ፧
entay tigebri alekhi?
WHICH?
ኣየናይ፧
(ayenay?)
Example Sentence
Which one is yours?
ኣየናይ ኢዩ ናትካ፧
ayenay iyu natka?
Which one is yours?
ኣየናይ ኢዩ ናትኪ፧
ayenay iyu natki?
HOW?
ከመይ፧
(kemey?)
Example Sentence
How are you?
ከመይ ኣለኻ፧
kemey alekha?
How are you?
ከመይ ኣለኺ፧
kemey alekhi?
HOW MUCH?
ክንደይ፧
(kindey?)
Example Sentence
How much is it?
ክንደይ'ዩ፧
kindey iyu?

💡 Grammar Insight

  • In Tigrinya, question words usually come right before the verb or at the very beginning of the sentence.
  • Important: Notice how the verb ending changes whether you are speaking to a male or a female!
