Lesson 3: Well-Being & Family

Learn to ask how someone is doing and inquire about their family members in Tigrinya.

Asking How Someone Is

How are you? (male)
ከመይ ኣሎኻ፧
Kemey alokha?
ከመይ ኣለኻ፧
Kemey alekha?
ከመ'ለኻ፧
Keme'lekha?
How are you? (female)
ከመይ ኣሎኺ
Kemey alokhi?
ከመይ ኣለኺ፧
Kemey alekhi?
ከሜ'ለኺ፧
Keme'lekhi?

Asking About Family

How is your sister? (asking a male)
ሓፍትኻ ከመይ ኣላ፧
Haftkha kemey ala?
ሓፍትኻ ከሜ'ላ፧
Haftkha keme'la?
How is your sister? (asking a female)
ሓፍቲኺ ከመይ ኣላ፧
Haftikhi kemey ala?
ሓፍቲኺ ከሜ'ላ፧
Haftikhi keme'la?
How is your brother? (asking a male)
ሓውኻ ከመይ ኣሎ፧
Hawkha kemey alo?
ሓውኻ ከመይ ኣሎ፧
Hawkha kemey alo?
ሓውኻ ከሜ'ሎ፧
Hawkha keme'lo?
How is your brother? (asking a female)
ሓውኺ ከመይ ኣሎ፧
Hawkhi kemey alo?
ሓውኺ ከመይ ኣሎ፧
Hawkhi kemey alo?
ሓውኺ ከሜ'ሎ፧
Hawkhi keme'lo?
How is your family? (asking a male)
ስድራኻ ከመይ ኣሎዉ፧
Sdrakha kemey alowu?
ስድራኻ ከመይ ኣለዉ፧
Sdrakha kemey alewu?
ስድራኻ ከመ'ለው፧
Sdrakha keme'lew?
How is your family? (asking a female)
ስድራኺ ከመይ ኣሎዉ፧
Sdrakhi kemey alowu?
ስድራኺ ከመይ ኣለዉ፧
Sdrakhi kemey alewu?
ስድራኺ ከሜ'ለው፧
Sdrakhi keme'lew?

💡 Grammar Insight: Plural Suffix

  • When asking about a group like "Family" (ስድራኻ), the verb ends in -wu / -ዉ for 3rd Person Plural: alewu.
