ቀስ ኢልካ ተዛረብ በጃኻ።
qes ilka tezareb bejaka.

💡 Grammar Insight: Addressing Genders

  • When asking a Male to speak slowly, use suffix -ka / -ኻ: qes ilka tezareb bejaka.
  • When asking a Female, it changes: qes ilki tzarebi bjaki.
