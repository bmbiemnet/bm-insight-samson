Lesson 8: Interpersonal Feelings

Learn how to express personal connections, likes, and dislikes toward others.

💡 Who Are You Talking To?

In Tigrinya, the ending of the verb changes depending on if the person you are talking to is Male or Female. Pay close attention to the end sounds: usually "-ka" (for males) and "-ki" (for females).

"I Like You"

How to say "I like you"
Option 1
እፈትወካ እየ
ifetweka iye
Option 2
ይፈትወካ እየ
yfetweka iye
Option 1
እፈትወኪ እየ

Option 2

"I Don't Like You"

How to say "I don't like you"

ኣይፈትወክን እየ
ayfetwekin iye

Asking the Question

"Do you like me?"
ትፈትወኒ'ዶ፧
tifetweni'do?
ትፈትውኒ'ዶ፧
tifetwini'do?
